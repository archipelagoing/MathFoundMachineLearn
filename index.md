## A place to build understanding

This site is a growing collection of notes, worked examples, and resources for studying the foundations of machine learning.

Topics to explore include:

- **Linear algebra:** vectors, matrices, eigenvalues, and decompositions.
- **Calculus:** derivatives, gradients, and multivariable functions.
- **Probability and statistics:** distributions, expectations, and inference.
- **Optimization:** objective functions, convexity, and gradient descent.

Use the sidebar to browse pages or search to find a topic.
